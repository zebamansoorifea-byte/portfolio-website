The Challenge

Aradhna Incense ek established dhoop sticks manufacturer aur exporter hai. Unka kaam tha — B2B distributors aur international buyers ke saath connect karna. Lekin bina proper website ke, potential partners pehle from aate hi nahi the. Unhe ek professional B2B presence chahiye thi jo trust build kare aur enquiries generate kare.

What I Built

Maine ek complete B2B website design aur develop ki — product catalog, export section, Private Label/OEM page, certifications display, aur B2B enquiry system ke saath. Brand identity bhi main hi banai — colors, typography, aur visual language jo manufacturers ke liye professional aur trustworthy feel karti hai.
