The Challenge

Samyak Computer Classes ek NSDC-certified institute hai Badarpur, Delhi mein — 250+ courses aur established reputation ke saath. Lekin unki digital presence almost non-existent thi. Koi modern website nahi, mobile pe properly nahi dikhte the, aur students direct WhatsApp pe enquire nahi kar pa rahe the.

What I Built

Main website ke saath saath, maine specifically designed landing pages banaye different courses ke liye — Digital Marketing, Accounting with AI, UI/UX Design, Data Science. Har cheez mobile-first design ke saath build ki gayi thi aur real lead generation ke liye optimize ki gayi thi.
